# Project Motive

## Purpose
To create a singular, infallible source of truth for organizational operations.

## Business Goal
Eliminate software licensing bloat by replacing Jira, Workday, Hubstaff, and Slack with a unified ecosystem tailored for the enterprise.

## Technical Goal
Demonstrate a scalable, event-driven microservices architecture capable of handling high-frequency telemetry and complex data relationships without degradation.

## Problems Being Solved
- Fragmented corporate data leading to poor executive decision-making.
- High latency in HR processing.
- Security vulnerabilities from having employees tracked across 5 different third-party SaaS vendors.

## Expected Impact
- **Business:** 30% reduction in SaaS expenditure, 50% increase in HR processing speed.
- **Technical:** A robust platform that serves as a foundation for internal corporate scaling.
